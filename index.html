Our Story

Why we started this

If you've ever driven to campus for a morning class, you know the routine: circle the Babbio Center Garage, climb level after level, and hope a spot opens up before you're late. There's no way to know if the garage is full until you're already inside it.

We're a group of Stevens sophomores in MGT 103, and we kept hearing the same frustration from classmates, commuters and staff. So we asked a simple question: what if you could check how many spots are open before you leave?

Our first prototype uses a phone camera at the garage entrance to count cars coming in and going out. That count updates a live number you can check on your phone. If it works, the next step is low-cost sensors at the entrance.
